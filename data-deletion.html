Data Deletion

Last updated 16 September 2026

This page explains how to have your BuilderPro OS data deleted, including any data we hold from an account you connected such as Facebook, Instagram, Google Business Profile, Google Ads, or Yelp.

Disconnect a single service yourself

This is the fastest route and takes effect immediately.

  1. Sign in to BuilderPro OS and open Marketing.
  2. Find the connected service on the Ads & Listings tab.
  3. Choose Disconnect.

Disconnecting deletes the stored access token for that service straight away, along with the cached numbers and reviews we pulled from it. We stop calling that provider at once. Nothing is posted or changed inside your Facebook, Google, or Yelp account itself.

Remove BuilderPro OS from Facebook

You can also revoke our access from Facebook's side. Open Settings & Privacy, then Settings, then Apps and Websites, select BuilderPro OS, and choose Remove. Facebook will notify us and we will delete the associated token and cached data.

Delete everything

To have your whole account and all associated data deleted, email privacy@builderpro-os.com from the address on your account with the subject Delete my data. Include your business name so we can locate the record.

We confirm receipt within 2 business days and complete deletion within 30 days. This removes your account, business profile, contacts, projects, invoices, scheduled posts, uploaded photos, and every stored third-party access token. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

What we may have to keep

We may retain a limited record of invoices and payments where tax or accounting law requires it, and we may keep records needed to resolve a dispute or meet a legal obligation. Anything kept for those reasons is isolated and is not used for any other purpose.

Requesting a copy instead

If you would like a copy of your data rather than deletion, write to the same address with the subject Export my data and we will send a machine-readable export.
