Supplier logo collector

Drop each chain’s logo into its slot. This names every file correctly, shows it at the exact size and on the exact tile BuilderPro draws it on, warns you when one will disappear, and packages the lot as a zip.

How to work through it
  1. Open the company’s own press or brand page — or its Wikipedia article, whose logo is usually clean and current — and save the logo.
  2. Drag the file onto its slot below. Or paste an image URL into the slot.
  3. Watch the small preview: that is the real 38 px tile. If it vanishes, it is a white logo — grab the dark or full-colour version instead.
  4. Hit Download zip and send it over. Nine is enough to start; the rest keep their lettermarks until they arrive.
