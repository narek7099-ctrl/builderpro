## 2. Who to go after

**Best fit:**
- Owner-operators and crews of 1 to 15.
- Project trades: roofing, remodeling, HVAC, plumbing, electrical, painting, concrete, landscaping, pools.
- Businesses where the owner is still answering the phone.

**Where to find them:**
- **Google Maps.** Search "[trade] near [town]". Skip the big companies with 200+ reviews and a call center. The sweet spot is 5 to 80 reviews and a website that looks home-made, or no website at all.
- **Facebook groups** for local contractors and homeowners. Watch for "does anyone know a good roofer?" posts. The contractors who answer are active and want work.
- **Yard signs and truck wraps** around town. The phone number is right there.

**The missed-call test** is your best opener:
1. Call the business after 5pm or on a Saturday.
2. If it goes to voicemail or nobody answers, write it down: date, time, what happened.
3. Use that fact in your call or email ("I called Saturday at 10 and got voicemail").
   - It's true, it's specific, and it shows the problem better than any pitch.
   - Don't leave a fake voicemail; just hang up, or leave your real one (script below).

**Keep a simple list.** One row per business, with these columns:
- Business, trade, town, phone, email, website
- Missed-call test: date, answered yes or no
- Contacted (date and how), reply, next step, status (new / talked / walkthrough / trial / client / not now)

## 3. Cold call script

Keep it under a minute unless they want to talk. Smile when you dial; it sounds different.

**Opener:**
> "Hi, is this [name]? This is [your name] with BuilderPro. Quick question, and I'll be fast: when you're up on a roof (or under a sink, or on a job) and your phone rings, who answers it?"

Let them answer. Usually it's "nobody", "voicemail" or "my wife".

**If they miss calls:**
> "That's what I hear from almost every contractor. Most homeowners who hit voicemail just call the next company. We built an AI receptionist named Lisa. She picks up every call, answers questions about your services, and books the estimate right into your calendar while you work. Want to hear her? Call (350) 333-1230 right now and talk to her like you're a customer. I'll wait, or I can call you back in five minutes."

**If they say they don't miss calls:**
> "Nice, that's rare. What about after hours and weekends? Lisa covers those, and she texts back anyone who hangs up. Most guys use her as the night and weekend shift."

**Questions to ask** (listen more than you talk):
- "How do leads come in right now: calls, texts, your website, Facebook?"
- "What do you use for estimates and invoices?"
- "How many people are on your crew?" Their answer tells you the plan:
  - just them: Foundation
  - 2 to 6 people: BuilderPro OS
  - more crews: Enterprise
- "If you could fix one thing about the office side of the business, what would it be?"

**Close for the next step.** Ask for a small yes, not the sale:
> "The easiest thing is a 20-minute walkthrough. I'll show you Lisa, an estimate going out, and what your customer sees. Does tomorrow or Thursday work better?"

If they'd rather try it themselves:
> "It's free for 14 days, no setup fee, no contract. Sign up at builderpro-os.com, and I'll text you the link so you have it."

Only text them if they said yes to that.

### Voicemail (20 seconds)

We have an AI receptionist that answers every call and books the job for you. Hear her yourself at (350) 333-1230. My number is [your number]. Thanks!"

## 5. Cold emails

**The rules for business email (the CAN-SPAM Act):**
- Use a real subject line.
- Say who you are.
- Include a postal address.
- Honor opt-outs right away.

Until the LLC has a business address, use the mailing address you're allowed to use, and add the footer below to every email. Send them one at a time from your own inbox, not as a blast. Personal emails get answers; blasts get marked as spam.

**Footer for every email:**
> [Your name] · BuilderPro · [mailing address]
> Not interested? Reply "no thanks" and I won't email again.

**Sequence:** email 1, then email 2 three days later, then email 3 a week after that. Stop as soon as they reply.

## 6. Texts

Text only people who have talked with you and agreed to a text, or who gave you their number for that. Cold texting cell phones can break the TCPA (federal law) and carrier rules. Calls and emails are the cold channels; texts are for follow-up.

**After a call where they said "text me the link":**
> Hi [name], it's [your name] from BuilderPro. Here's the link we talked about: [trade page link]. Lisa's demo line is (350) 333-1230. 14 days free, no setup fee.

**The day before a walkthrough:**
> Hi [name], [your name] from BuilderPro. Just confirming our walkthrough tomorrow at [time]. Reply here if you need a different time.

**After a walkthrough:**
> Thanks for your time today, [name]! Here's where to start your free trial: builderpro-os.com/signup.html. If you get stuck on anything, text me here.

**During the trial (day 3):**
> Hi [name], how's BuilderPro going? Has Lisa answered any calls yet? Happy to help set anything up.

**Day 12 of the trial:**
> Hi [name], your BuilderPro trial ends in 2 days. Anything you want to change before then? If it's not for you, you can cancel in Settings and you won't be charged.

## 8. The 20-minute walkthrough

1. **Their business (3 min).** Ask how leads come in and what happens when they miss a call. Write down their words; you'll use them.
2. **Lisa (5 min).** Put your phone on speaker and call (350) 333-1230. Let them ask her a question like a customer would. Then show the missed-call text-back.
3. **Estimate to payment (5 min).** Build a quick Good / Better / Best estimate. Show what the homeowner sees on their phone: approve, sign, pay.
4. **Their day (4 min).** Show the phone app home screen, today's jobs, a crew login, and the calculator on their trade's page.
5. **Next step (3 min).**
   - Recommend one plan based on crew size, and say why in one sentence.
   - Then: "Want to start the 14 days free now? I'll stay on while you sign up."

Don't demo everything. Show the 3 things that fix the problem they told you about in step 1.

## 9. Habits that win

- **Aim for 20 calls or emails a day, every weekday.** It's a numbers game for the first 10 clients.
- **Answer every reply within the hour.** You're selling speed, so be fast.
- **Ask every new client "who else do you know?"** Contractors know other contractors in different trades.
- **When a client is happy, ask them for a short Google review of BuilderPro, or a one-line quote** to put on the site, with their permission.
