# BuilderPro launch video (about 90 seconds)

The story: BuilderPro was built to help growing contractors grow and run their company
without paying huge amounts up front. We asked about 100 contractors what their biggest
problems were, then built the answers into one app.

**Who's on camera:** an AI-generated spokesperson who speaks for the company
(FoundationTech LLC / BuilderPro), not as the founder and not as a contractor. You perform each
spokesperson scene yourself; Higgsfield replaces you with the spokesperson, changes the
background and outfit, and changes your voice to theirs.

**Rules for the spokesperson (keep these, they protect the company):**
- Speaks as the company ("we built", "BuilderPro does"). Never a personal backstory
  ("I've been a roofer for 20 years", "I use this in my business").
- Never plays a customer and never gives a testimonial. Real customer quotes only, once we have them.
- The face is invented and must not look like a real person.
- When uploading, turn on the platform's "AI-generated / altered content" label (YouTube,
  Instagram/Facebook, TikTok all have one) and add "Presenter is AI-generated" to the description.

**Things to confirm before filming** (marked ✱ in the script):
- ✱1 The problems contractors actually told you, in their words if possible. The script uses
  missed calls, slow estimates and getting paid as placeholders. Change them to what you really heard.
- ✱2 "About 100 contractors": keep the exact wording you're comfortable standing behind.
- ✱3 Lisa's scene: live call (HighLevel on for the filming week) or the text-back conversation
