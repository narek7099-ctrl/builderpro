# BuilderPro demo video (about 75 seconds)

A screen recording plus your voice. Use it on the homepage, in email 1 of the sales kit, and on social media.

**What to record with:**
- **Phone:** the built-in screen recorder (iPhone: Control Center > Screen Recording, hold it down to turn the mic on).
- **Computer:** a free recorder (QuickTime on a Mac; on Windows, press Win + Alt + R to record with the Xbox Game Bar).
- Record each scene as its own clip. Then join the clips in CapCut or iMovie (both free) and add captions. Most people watch with the sound off.

**Before you record:**
- Use a demo account with sample customers and jobs labeled "(sample)". Never show a real customer's name, phone number or address.
- Turn on Do Not Disturb so no notifications pop up.
- Don't pay with a real card on camera. Stripe is live, so a real payment would really charge someone. Show the payment page, then cut.
- Lisa's demo line is (350) 333-1230. A real call to it is fine.

## Scene 1: the problem (0:00 to 0:08)

**Screen:** a phone ringing on a job site, or text on a plain background: "You're on a roof. Your phone rings."

**Voice:**
> "You're on a job and your phone rings. You can't pick up. That customer just called the next contractor."

**Caption:** Missed call = missed job

## Scene 2: Lisa answers (0:08 to 0:28)

**Screen:** call (350) 333-1230 on speaker and record about 15 seconds of the call. Ask something a homeowner would ask: "Hi, my roof is leaking near the chimney, can someone come look at it this week?"

**Voice (over the end of the call):**
> "With BuilderPro, Lisa answers. She's an AI receptionist who knows your services, answers questions and books the visit right into your calendar, 24/7."

**Caption:** Lisa answers every call, 24/7

## Scene 3: the missed-call text (0:28 to 0:36)

**Screen:** the BuilderPro inbox showing a missed-call text-back conversation (sample).

**Voice:**
> "If someone hangs up, she texts them back in seconds, so the lead doesn't go cold."

**Caption:** Every missed call gets a text back

## Scene 4: estimate to payment (0:36 to 0:58)

**Screen, in order:**
1. In the app, build an estimate with Good / Better / Best options (sample customer). Show 2 or 3 taps, not every step.
2. Tap Send.
3. Switch to the customer's view of the estimate link on a phone. Show them picking an option and signing.
4. Show the payment screen, then cut before entering a card.

**Voice:**
> "Send a good, better, best estimate from your phone. Your customer picks one, signs, and pays from the same link. The money goes straight to your account."

**Caption:** Estimate → signed → paid

## Scene 5: your day in one place (0:58 to 1:08)

**Screen:** the phone home screen (today's jobs, leads, the bottom bar). Then a quick look at a job with photos and the crew.

**Voice:**
> "Your jobs, your crew and your numbers live in one app, on your phone or your computer."

**Caption:** Jobs, crew and money in one app
