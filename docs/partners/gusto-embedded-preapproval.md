## Why payroll
Our customers already log their crews' hours in BuilderPro, by job, from the crew phone
app (clock in and out on site). Today they re-type those hours into a separate payroll
service, or pay by hand-written check with no tax withheld. We want them to run correct
payroll where the hours already are.

## What we would build
- Company onboarding inside BuilderPro (Gusto Embedded Flows or API)
- Employee and contractor onboarding: workers add W-4, state forms and bank details themselves
- Run payroll from BuilderPro's time entries: review screen, then submit with a two-step confirmation
- Pay stubs shown in BuilderPro (and Gusto's official copies); printable paychecks for workers paid by check
- Contractor (1099) payments
- Labor cost posted to each job, so owners see true job profit
